Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots activate Marcus Bryant from IR, release Kindle Vildor

    The Patriots have activated offensive tackle Marcus Bryant from injured reserve while releasing cornerback Kindle Vildor to create space on the roster for upcoming moves, potentially in light of player recoveries. This strategic move raises questions about the future of the depth chart, especially as the team anticipates the return of others from IR such as A.J. Brown and Mack Hollins.

  • Hunter Henry injury adds to Patriots' depth woes

    Tight end Hunter Henry left the Week 8 game against the Dolphins with a foot injury and did not return, adding to the Patriots' long list of injuries.
