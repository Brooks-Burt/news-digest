  • Patriots trade Kayshon Boutte to Texans for safety Jaylen Reed, draft pick

    The New England Patriots have traded wide receiver Kayshon Boutte to the Houston Texans, acquiring safety Jaylen Reed and a 2028 seventh-round pick in return. This move signals a shift in the wide receiver room as Boutte's role was diminished following the arrivals of A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs. Reports indicate that Boutte was dissatisfied with his role, prompting the trade to become a win-win for both teams.

Standout Performers

  • Behren Morton impresses in preseason win over Eagles

    Behren Morton had a standout performance against the Eagles, completing 13 of 17 passes for 129 yards and throwing a game-winning touchdown to Nick DeGennaro. Additionally, he contributed as an emergency punter, showcasing his versatility. Morton's efforts bolster his case for a roster spot as the season approaches.
