Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots rookie Caleb Lomu is impressing in training camp, causing depth chart speculation.

    Caleb Lomu, the New England Patriots' first-round pick and rookie offensive tackle, is not only being trained at right tackle but is also showing versatility by working as a guard and jumbo tight end. His strong development could lead to a shift in the offensive line, potentially making veteran Morgan Moses expendable. Veteran players, such as Alijah Vera-Tucker, have praised his attitude and hard work, emphasizing he has already shown considerable skill and the ability to dominate in drills.

  • Mike Vrabel provides an update on Gabe Jacas' recovery progress.

    Head coach Mike Vrabel expressed optimism about rookie edge rusher Gabe Jacas, indicating he is progressing well in his recovery from a knee procedure. Jacas, who recently signed his rookie deal, has not yet participated in training but is expected to return sooner rather than later, which would significantly bolster the Patriots' thin pass-rushing unit that has been struggling with injuries.
