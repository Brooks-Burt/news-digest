  • Romeo Doubs embraces Patriots' WR competition and sets high standards

Contracts & Business

  • Stefon Diggs signs a one-year, $12 million deal with Commanders

    Stefon Diggs has signed a one-year contract worth up to $12 million with the Washington Commanders, following his release from the Patriots earlier this offseason. This move signals the end of speculation regarding a potential return to New England after the Patriots brought in A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs, leaving questions about the team’s decision to cut Diggs when he provided vital offensive production last season, including 1,013 yards receiving.

Injuries

  • Gabe Jacas returns to training camp after contract delay and surgery

    Rookie Gabe Jacas has officially joined Patriots training camp following a prolonged absence caused by contract negotiations and a knee procedure. He expressed determination to prove his worth as he integrates into the defensive scheme, aiming to make up for lost time. Coach Mike Vrabel has noted the importance of Jacas adapting quickly, especially with fellow edge rusher Harold Landry still on the physically unable to perform list. His presence will be crucial for the depth and effectiveness of the pass rush moving forward.

Standout Performers

  • Drake Maye impresses during training camp, leads offense to strong finish

    Despite a shaky start, quarterback Drake Maye concluded his latest training camp session by completing 11 of his last 12 passes, showcasing his resilience and leadership. Maye's performance included several key completions to A.J. Brown and Mack Hollins, underscoring the growing chemistry within the offense, which is crucial as the Patriots prepare for challenges ahead in the 2026 season. His ability to rebound from adversity is a promising sign for the Patriots' offensive momentum going forward.

Coaching & Scheme

  • Christian Gonzalez thriving in practice against A.J. Brown

    A.J. Brown’s addition to the Patriots is proving beneficial for both him and second-year cornerback Christian Gonzalez, who benefits from the high-level competition during training camp. The fierce one-on-one matchups between Brown and Gonzalez are helping both players develop, setting a competitive tone crucial for a successful team culture under head coach Mike Vrabel. This development highlights the Patriots' strategy of incorporating elite talents to enhance the overall skill level of the roster.
