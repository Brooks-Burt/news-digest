Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots place Romeo Doubs on IR, impacting WR depth

    The Patriots have officially placed wide receiver Romeo Doubs on injured reserve, sidelining him for a minimum of four games due to a shoulder injury sustained while catching a touchdown against the Jets. His absence leaves A.J. Brown, DeMario Douglas, Efton Chism III, and Kyle Williams in the receiving corps, making Brown's performance increasingly critical as he returns from his own injury.
