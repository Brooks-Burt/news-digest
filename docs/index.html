  • Drake Maye breaks down the Patriots wide receiver room

Injuries

  • A.J. Brown suffers dislocated thumb at practice

    A.J. Brown dislocated his left thumb during Monday’s training camp practice but continued to participate afterward. The injury occurred during a 1-on-1 drill against Christian Gonzalez, but Brown returned to the field shortly after and finished practice with several touchdowns, showcasing his determination to remain active despite the injury. This moment raises concerns about his health as training camp progresses.
