  • A.J. Brown suffers high ankle sprain, placed on injured reserve

    Wide receiver A.J. Brown will miss at least four games due to a high ankle sprain he sustained in the opener against the Seahawks. This injury adds pressure on the Patriots' receiving corps, as they will have to adapt without their top target for the next several weeks.
