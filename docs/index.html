  • Drake Maye receives praise as potential top quarterback

    Drake Maye, the New England Patriots' starting quarterback, has been rated as the second-best first-round pick since 2020 by CBS Sports' Mike Renner. Maye's performance has been impressive, leading a mediocre roster to a Super Bowl appearance in his second season. Analysts believe his success hinges on the support he receives from his team, particularly from the front office in building a strong supporting cast around him, which has raised questions as to whether the Patriots have done enough to bolster their roster this offseason. This spotlight on Maye highlights the need for the team to effectively manage and develop his talent as they head into the 2026 season.
