Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots trade Kayshon Boutte to Texans

    The New England Patriots have traded wide receiver Kayshon Boutte to the Houston Texans, receiving safety Jaylen Reed and a 2028 seventh-round pick in return. With the addition of A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs, Boutte's role was expected to diminish significantly, prompting the move to alleviate a logjam at wideout and add much-needed safety depth. The decision was also influenced by Boutte's request for a trade earlier in the offseason, signaling a lack of faith in his future with the team.

Injuries

  • TreVeyon Henderson injured in practice

    Running back TreVeyon Henderson left practice early after tweaking his ankle during drills. While the severity of the injury remains unclear, he will miss at least one practice. This development opens the door for backup running backs, including Jam Miller and Lan Larison, to increase their chances of making the roster ahead of the preseason finale against the Browns.
