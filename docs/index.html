  • Patriots trade OL Caedan Wallace to Dolphins

    New England shipped offensive lineman Caedan Wallace to the Miami Dolphins for a sixth-round pick in 2028, along with a seventh-round pick in 2029. This trade marked a clear shift as the team let go of one of its 2024 draft choices, which has underperformed since being selected 68th overall. It also reflects their continued emphasis on strengthening the offensive line with other recent draft picks.

  • Hunter Henry agrees to a two-year extension with the Patriots

    Hunter Henry has signed a two-year extension with the Patriots, worth up to $20 million, ensuring that the veteran tight end remains with the team through the 2028 season.
