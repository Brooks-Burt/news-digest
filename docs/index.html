Standout Performers

  • Lan Larison Shines in Running Back Competition

    Lan Larison put forth a strong performance during the preseason game against the Eagles, totaling 128 all-purpose yards and scoring a touchdown, showcasing his versatility as a runner, receiver, and returner. His gritty play earned him praise from head coach Mike Vrabel, solidifying his case for the RB3 position behind Rhamondre Stevenson and TreVeyon Henderson as the competition heats up.

  • Kindle Vildor Emerges as Cornerback Contender
