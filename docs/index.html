Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots finalize initial 53-man roster

    The New England Patriots announced their official 53-man roster just ahead of the NFL cutdown deadline, marking a significant reduction from their training camp roster. Among the key moves was trading for running back Corey Kiner and claiming tight end Cameron Latu and linebacker Darius Muasau from waivers, while notable cuts included veteran linebacker K.J. Britt and center Ben Brown, who was released to create space for these additions.

  • Kayshon Boutte traded to Texans

    The Patriots traded wide receiver Kayshon Boutte to the Houston Texans for safety Jaylen Reed and a 2028 sixth-round pick, signaling a shift in the team's receiver depth after opting to keep younger talents. Though Boutte showed promise, his crowded position ultimately led to the decision to let him go.

    After the roster cut decisions, head coach Mike Vrabel addressed the media, discussing the team's depth issues and the tough nature of cuts. He expressed confidence that all healthy players, including Christian Gonzalez amidst contract negotiations, would be available for the season opener.

Injuries

  • Brenden Schooler placed on injured reserve

    The Patriots will begin the 2026 season without special teams ace Brenden Schooler, who has been placed on injured reserve due to an undisclosed injury that has sidelined him throughout the summer. He'll be eligible to return after missing at least four games, raising concerns over the team's special teams depth.

  • Harold Landry to begin season on PUP list

    Edge rusher Harold Landry will start the regular season on the physically unable to perform list, missing at least the first four games as he recovers from a knee injury. This development further complicates a thin pass rush situation for the Patriots heading into the season.
