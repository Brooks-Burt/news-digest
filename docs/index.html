Roster & Depth Chart

  • Mike Vrabel and Eliot Wolf are credited for the Patriots' quick roster rebuild

    Head coach Mike Vrabel and general manager Eliot Wolf are being recognized for rapidly improving the Patriots' roster. After finishing with a 4-13 record in 2025, they have made significant additions, highlighted by quarterback Drake Maye and wide receiver A.J. Brown, and seen their roster rank as the 10th best heading into the 2026 season. This dynamic duo’s strategy in the offseason has raised expectations for a team keen on competing for a Super Bowl title once again.

Injuries

  • Injuries are a concern for the Patriots as training camp progresses

    During recent practices, cornerback Carlton Davis and Charles Woods have suffered injuries, creating further depth issues at the cornerback position for the Patriots. Additionally, running back Rhamondre Stevenson has missed practice due to a heat-related issue. These injuries raise concerns about the team's defensive depth as they prepare for the upcoming season.
