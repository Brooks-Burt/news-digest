Injuries

  • Injuries will force Patriots to make roster-defining decisions the rest of 2026 season

    The Patriots have placed over 20 players on injured reserve this season, significantly impacting their roster depth. WR Romeo Doubs and OL Alijah Vera-Tucker are the latest to join the IR, and the team will need to strategically activate a limited number of players before season's end. Decisions will be complicated by the ongoing injuries and lack of depth in crucial areas, particularly on the offensive line and at wide receiver.

Standout Performers

  • Eli Raridon is quietly becoming exactly what the Patriots needed at tight end

    Rookie tight end Eli Raridon is emerging as a reliable target for quarterback Drake Maye, notching his second touchdown of the season against the Bears. He’s shown significant promise with 168 yards on 12 receptions, stepping up particularly during a time of receiver injuries. As he gains more snaps and continues to produce, Raridon could be a key part of the Patriots' offense going forward.

Coaching & Scheme

  • Mike Vrabel's anti-Bill Belichick approach with Patriots rookies is working like a charm

    Mike Vrabel’s coaching style is giving rookies the room to grow through mistakes, contrasting sharply with his predecessor Bill Belichick’s stricter methods. This approach is particularly evident with Eli Raridon and Gabe Jacas, who are showing promise despite earlier issues. Vrabel’s willingness to allow young players to learn on the job could pay dividends as the season progresses.

Other News

  • Bears HC compliments Drake Maye on primetime performance: ‘He’s an outstanding player’

    Despite the loss to the Bears, Drake Maye caught the attention of Chicago's head coach Ben Johnson, who praised his dual-threat ability and competitive spirit. Maye had a solid outing, with 321 passing yards and two touchdowns, though he did throw a costly red-zone interception. Johnson believes Maye has caught his rhythm this season and could be a formidable QB for the Patriots moving forward.
