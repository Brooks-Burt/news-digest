  • Kurt Warner thinks Patriots’ lack of ‘pressure plan’ doomed Drake Maye vs. Bears

    Hall of Famer Kurt Warner criticized the Patriots for failing to implement a 'pressure plan' during their loss to the Bears, which hindered Drake Maye's performance, especially against a strong defensive front. Warner highlighted that without pre-snap adjustments against blitzes, the offense struggles to execute effectively, putting undue pressure on the young quarterback.

  • Gabe Jacas has gone from Patriots concern to potential Rookie of the Year threat

    Rookie edge defender Gabe Jacas has turned heads since the start of the season, amassing significant pressure stats including leading all edge defenders in pass rush win rate. Despite a stumble early in camp, Jacas is now recognized as a standout performer and a potential contender for Defensive Rookie of the Year honors, showing great promise in his first NFL season.
