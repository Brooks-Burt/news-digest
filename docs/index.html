Roster & Depth Chart

  • Kyle Williams faces uphill battle at receiver position

    New England Patriots wide receiver Kyle Williams is currently slotted as the team's WR7, trailing behind A.J. Brown, Romeo Doubs, Kayshon Boutte, Mack Hollins, DeMario Douglas, and Efton Chism. Reported struggles in training camp indicate Williams has work to do to secure a place in the rotation, despite having played all 17 games last season without making a start. His performance in upcoming practices will be crucial for his standing on the depth chart.

  • Christian Gonzalez's potential record-setting contract offer

    Owner Robert Kraft revealed that the Patriots have extended an offer to cornerback Christian Gonzalez that would make him the highest-paid cornerback in NFL history. This comes amid ongoing negotiations after the team exercised his fifth-year option earlier this offseason.
