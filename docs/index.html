Roster & Depth Chart

  • Patriots sign rookie linebacker Gabe Jacas

    The New England Patriots signed rookie linebacker Gabe Jacas to a four-year contract worth up to $8.6 million, resolving the lengthy absence related to his unsigned status. Jacas, a second-round pick, is expected to bolster the team's depth at edge rusher, especially with Harold Landry on the PUP list.

  • Patriots claim speedy wide receiver Kobe Prentice from Panthers

    The New England Patriots have claimed wide receiver Kobe Prentice off waivers from the Carolina Panthers, adding him to their crowded receiver room. The 5-foot-10 Prentice, who ran a 4.37-second 40-yard dash, is likely to provide depth at the slot position.

Injuries

  • Harold Landry opens training camp on PUP list

    Starting outside linebacker Harold Landry was placed on the Physically Unable to Perform (PUP) list at the start of training camp due to an ongoing knee issue, raising concerns about his availability for the regular season. Head coach Mike Vrabel declined to predict when Landry might return to practice.

  • Robert Kraft announces contract offer to Christian Gonzalez

    Patriots owner Robert Kraft revealed that the team has extended an offer to cornerback Christian Gonzalez that would make him the highest-paid cornerback in NFL history, also setting a franchise record.
